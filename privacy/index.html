BUNN Labs, Inc. (“BUNN”, “we”, “us”) builds payment software — stablecoin payment links, apps for businesses and the people who pay them, and point-of-sale terminals (together, the “Services”) — and runs the website bunn.world. This policy explains what personal information we handle, why we handle it, who we share it with, and the rights you have.

We act in two different roles, and the difference decides whom you should ask about your information.

As a controller

We decide how and why we use your account data, app analytics, device and NFC data, marketing data, and what you send us through our website.

For this information, ask us.

As a collector for our partners

We collect identity-verification (KYC and KYB) and transaction information and pass it to regulated partners who open and operate your accounts. Each partner is an independent controller of that information under its own privacy notice.

For this information, ask the partner.

02Which laws apply

Which data-protection laws apply depends on where you are and how you use the Services:

  • UAE Personal Data Protection Law — Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (the “PDPL”). Our Services are built for the UAE, and we apply the PDPL to information about people in the UAE.
  • DIFC Data Protection Law — DIFC Law No. 5 of 2020. It applies where personal information is processed in or from the Dubai International Financial Centre, including by any partner established there.
  • EU and UK GDPR — Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and its United Kingdom equivalent, if you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom.
  • Other local laws — including the consumer privacy laws of US states, where they apply to you.

03Information we control

Account data

When you create an account, we collect your name, date of birth, phone number, email address and residential address, and the one-time codes we send to confirm your contact details. You sign in with a passkey: we store only its public key, and its private key never leaves your device. For business accounts, we also collect the company’s name, your role and your business contact details.

App analytics

We may collect information about how you use our apps — such as the screens and features you use, the steps you complete, session times, the app version, crash and performance reports, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We use it to understand how the Services perform and to improve them. Where the law requires your consent for analytics, we ask for it first.

Device and NFC data

We collect device identifiers, the device model, operating system and app version, your IP address, and language and time-zone settings. On BUNN point-of-sale terminals, we also process the terminal’s identifier and status and — when a terminal writes a payment link to an NFC tag — the tag’s identifier and the link written to it. We use this to run and secure the Services, route payments to the right business and prevent fraud.

Marketing data

We collect business contact details — name, job title, company, work email and phone number — that you give us or that we obtain from public and professional sources, such as company websites and professional networks. When we email you, we may record whether the email was delivered, opened or clicked. We use this to tell businesses about BUNN. Every marketing email lets you opt out, and you can also write to compliance@bunn.world.

Website and enquiry data

When you contact us through our website, we collect your name, position, work email, phone number, type of business and the page you contacted us from. We store your enquiry in our database and forward it by email to our team so we can reply.

Our websites do not use cookies, analytics tools or advertising pixels. When you load a page, our hosting provider processes technical data — such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request — to deliver and protect the site. Your browser also fetches fonts and icons from third-party providers (Google Fonts, Fontshare and an open-source flag-icon library), which receive your IP address as part of those requests.

Communications

If you email or message us, we keep the messages and our replies so we can help you and keep a record of our conversations.

04Information we collect for our partners

To open and operate your accounts and move money on your behalf, we work with regulated partners. We collect the information they need through our apps and pass it to them:

  • Identity verification (KYC) — your name, date of birth, nationality, residential address and contact details; identity documents such as a passport or Emirates ID; and a selfie or short video used to confirm that the document belongs to you, which may involve facial comparison.
  • Business verification (KYB) — the company’s legal name, registration details and documents, registered address, ownership structure, ultimate beneficial owners, directors and authorised signatories.
  • Transaction data — amounts, currencies and digital assets, wallet addresses, bank details, counterparties, references, transaction hashes and settlement details.
  • Fund data — where you invest through a fund, your investment instructions, holdings and related records.

Each partner is an independent controller. Once a partner receives this information, it decides how and why it uses it — for example, to verify your identity, screen for sanctions and money-laundering risks, open and operate your accounts, and keep the records its regulators require — under its own privacy notice. We don’t control that processing. To ask about it, or to exercise your rights over information a partner holds, contact the partner using the details in its notice. If you’re not sure whom to ask, write to us and we’ll point you to the right place.

We keep what we need to run your BUNN account — such as your verification status and the transactions shown in the app — and we never use your identity documents for marketing.

Your consent at the handoff

Before we send your identity documents to a partner, the app asks you to confirm:

“By continuing, your identity documents will be shared with regulated partners to open your accounts and operate your fund on your behalf.”

If you choose not to continue, we don’t share them — but we can’t open your account without them. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to compliance@bunn.world. That stops future sharing. It doesn’t undo sharing that has already taken place, and a partner may have to keep the information it holds to meet its own legal obligations.

06Who else we share it with

Apart from our partners, we share personal information only:

  • With service providers that work for us — including Vercel (website hosting), Supabase (databases), Google Workspace (email), Resend (email delivery), and providers of analytics, error-reporting and customer-support tools. They may process information only on our instructions and under contract.
  • With authorities — regulators, law enforcement or courts, where the law requires it, or where it’s necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, BUNN or others.
  • In a business transaction — with a buyer, investor or successor in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, who will be bound by this policy.
  • With your permission, or at your direction.

We don’t sell personal information, and we don’t share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

07International transfers

BUNN Labs is based in the United States, and our Services are built mainly for people in the UAE. Your information will therefore move from the UAE and other countries to the United States, and to the countries where our partners and service providers operate. These countries may not have data-protection laws equivalent to yours, so we protect transfers as the law requires:

  • From the UAE, we transfer information to countries the PDPL recognises as providing adequate protection. Otherwise, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as contractual protections, on the transfer being necessary to perform our contract with you, or on your consent.
  • From the DIFC, we rely on an adequacy decision of the DIFC Commissioner of Data Protection, or on appropriate safeguards such as the DIFC standard contractual clauses.
  • From the EEA or the UK, we rely on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses (with the UK addendum), or on another transfer mechanism the law recognises.

Write to compliance@bunn.world for details of the safeguards that apply to your information.

08How long we keep it

  • Account data — while your account is open, and afterwards for as long as we need it to resolve disputes, enforce our agreements and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.
  • App analytics, device and NFC data — only for as long as it’s useful to operate and improve the Services, after which we delete or aggregate it.
  • Marketing data — until you opt out. After that, we keep only what we need to make sure we don’t contact you again.
  • Website enquiries and communications — as long as we need them to respond and to manage any relationship that follows.

Partners keep the information they control under their own notices. Anti-money-laundering rules typically require them to keep records for at least five years after a relationship ends.

09How we protect it

  • Our websites and Services use encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Access to personal information is limited to the people who need it for their work. Our public website can submit enquiries to our database but cannot read them back.
  • Credentials and keys are stored encrypted and kept out of our public code.
  • Passkeys keep the secret part of your sign-in on your own device.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we’ll tell you and the relevant authorities where the law requires.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct information that’s inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your information;
  • restrict how we use it, or object to our using it — including for direct marketing, which we’ll always stop on request;
  • send your information to you, or to another organisation, in a portable format;
  • not subject you to decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. This doesn’t affect processing that has already taken place.

To make a request, email compliance@bunn.world. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we’ll reply within the time the law allows. For information a partner controls, contact the partner — see Our partners.

You also have the right to complain to a data-protection authority: in the UAE, the UAE Data Office; in the DIFC, the Commissioner of Data Protection; in the EEA, your local supervisory authority; and in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office. We’d appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, you have the rights that law gives you, and we won’t discriminate against you for using them.

11Children

The Services are for businesses and for adults aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect information about children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

12Changes to this policy

We’ll update this policy when our practices, our partners or the law change, and we’ll change the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, we’ll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
