09Risks

Stablecoins and digital payments carry real risks, and you could lose money:

  • Not a bank deposit. Stablecoins and balances held with Partners are not bank deposits and are not covered by deposit insurance or any government guarantee.
  • Peg risk. A stablecoin can trade away from its target value, and its issuer can fail or suspend redemptions.
  • Partner risk. A Partner may fail, become insolvent, lose its licence or suspend services. Your protection then depends on its safeguarding arrangements and the law that applies to it, not on BUNN.
  • Blockchain risk. Transactions can be irreversible. Networks can congest, fork or fail, and fees can change quickly. A payment sent to the wrong address usually can’t be recovered.
  • Fund and return risk. The value of a fund can fall as well as rise. Any return is variable, not guaranteed, and can fall to zero.
  • Regulatory risk. Laws on digital assets change, and a service may become unavailable where you are.
  • Tax. Your transactions may have tax consequences. Get your own tax advice.
